What I actually do

UX strategy

Research and data become a direction teams can act on. I map the journeys, then tie experience quality to metrics the business already watches — CSATs on real flows, folded into company OKRs, so design stops arguing from taste.

Design operations

Workflows, rituals, governance, documentation. The unglamorous plumbing that makes delivery predictable and lets designers spend their hours designing instead of negotiating process.

Design systems

Multi-brand components, tokens, clear ownership. Grown incrementally and adopted in daily work, because a system nobody uses is a documentation project with better typography.

Growing teams

Hiring, onboarding, mentoring, career paths. T-shaped profiles and a skill matrix so the team keeps its depth while it gains range — and so growth does not cost cohesion.
