I

Where I add value

Four things teams consistently bring me in to do — and the reason they keep me close to both strategy and delivery.

i

UX strategy

Turning research and data into a direction a team can act on. I define the vision, map the journeys, and tie experience quality to numbers the business already watches.

CSAT → OKRSatisfaction became a tracked company objective at coches.net, not a survey nobody read.

ii

Design operations

Removing the friction between design, product and engineering — workflows, rituals, governance and documentation that make delivery predictable, so designers spend their time designing.

3 marketsOne way of working across Spain, France and Germany, with local teams still able to move.

iii

Design systems

Systems teams actually adopt. Multi-brand components, tokens, clear ownership and light governance — grown incrementally instead of shipped as a framework nobody uses.

7 brandsFotocasa, Habitaclia, coches.net, Milanuncios, InfoJobs, Leboncoin and Kleinanzeigen on one shared foundation.

iv

Leading and growing teams

Hiring, onboarding, mentoring and career paths. T-shaped profiles and an explicit skill matrix, so depth and versatility stay in balance while the team scales.

2 → 8A UX team grown fourfold and kept together — the part of the record I am most careful about.
