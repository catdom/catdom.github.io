What this borrows, and what it does not

The list is also a chart.

  • Taken from the reference: the accent plate, the numbered rows, the idea that only the active row is fully lit while the rest sit back.
  • Not taken: the vertical spine with the names hung to its right. Here the spine is horizontal, because the section's subject is time — a single 2005 → 2026 axis at the top, with a bar under each role positioned on it. The shape of the career is legible before a word is read: nine years at one company, then four, then four.
  • Kept from the site: the twelve-column rig, drawn in white so the grid crosses onto the blue; the mono metadata voice; DM Sans for the names; Geist for the prose; the dimension callouts as boxed numbers.
  • Interaction: hovering or tabbing a row deepens its ground, brings its name to full white, fills its bar and opens the summary beside it. On a phone every row is open and the bars stay — no hover, no hidden content.
