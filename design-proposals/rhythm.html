Grounds · three options

Light, dark, repeat

The same five sections three times over, with only the ground of each one changed. The footer counts as a section, which is what makes the count odd and forces a choice: either the page ends dark, or the alternation breaks somewhere on purpose.

Option A

Paper · accent · paper · accent · dark What the site does today, carried through: the blue plates alternate with paper and the footer closes on ink. Three grounds, so nothing repeats twice in a row.

What actually changes, beyond the colour

  • The cards have to pick a side. On the accent they stay white and keep their own ink, which is what the site does now. On ink they have to become dark panels — a white card on a dark ground reads as a hole, and four of them read as a lightbox.
  • The blue stops being loud on ink. #1f3fd8 on #14130f is 2.4:1 — unreadable as text. On a dark ground the accent has to lift to something like #7d92f0 (7.1:1), which is a second accent to maintain. Option B pays that cost once; Option C pays it on every dark section.
  • Five sections, two grounds, no clean alternation. Whatever you pick, one boundary repeats or the page ends where it started. A is the safest, B is the most disciplined, C is the one with a point of view.
  • The 24px strip under the marquee gets more interesting. It shows the next section's ground, so on B and C it is the moment the page goes dark — visible before you scroll into it.
