## The problem

Spatial interfaces break the assumptions of flat motion design: there is no
fixed viewport, and easing curves tuned for a screen read as sluggish in depth.

## Approach

A documented motion language built on physical rather than temporal
specification — mass, damping and travel distance instead of duration curves.
Components were authored in Rive so designers could tune behaviour directly.

## Outcome

A shared vocabulary that survived the handoff to two other teams.
